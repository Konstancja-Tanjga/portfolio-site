## Profile

I own products end to end, from the first interview to the shipped screen. Ten years in complex, data-rich software: enterprise ERP, insurance and healthcare, legal and regulatory, industrial energy. Currently Lead Product Designer for APplus ERP, ERP System of the Year 2025 in the UX category, a mature multi-module platform used across DACH on desktop and mobile. I designed four of its products from zero, built the design system they run on, and run my own research with customers and domain experts.

## How I work

1. Discovery and research. Interviews, observation, surveys and domain-expert sessions. The problem before the screen.
2. Design in Figma. IA and flows, then UI built from the shared library: components, variants, variables and tokens.
3. Prototype and validate. Interactive prototypes tested with real users in moderated sessions and beta programmes.
4. Handoff and delivery. Dev Mode, Code Connect and Storybook for engineering, then design review of what gets built.

Underneath all four: the design system lives in code as versioned packages on the internal Nexus registry, with Storybook on Chromatic as the living reference that design and engineering both work from.

**Elly, the platform's first AI assistant, released with APplus 9, 04/2025.** New product, designed from zero.
- The first AI surface in the platform, desktop and mobile: streaming answers over the documentation, with citations, error states and handoff to a person. The states that decide trust, not the chat bubble.
- My study with 13 consultants and solution architects changed the roadmap: 12 of 13 would use it again, but trust scored lowest of every dimension, so citations shipped; and 8 of 13 had never seen the assistant at all, a discovery problem rather than a usability one.

- FOX design system. Built from scratch and led its migration to v3 across product lines: tokens, components, patterns and templates for six consuming products. I review component changes with engineering, so what the designers use in Figma is what ships.
- Design practice and strategy. Represent design in roadmap and strategy discussions; run alignment workshops that turn conflicting stakeholder requests into testable decisions. Where a flow is easier to show than to describe, I hand over a working prototype alongside the Figma file.
