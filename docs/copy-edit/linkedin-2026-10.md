## About (2 600 characters)

I lead design for APplus ERP at Asseco Solutions, a platform for mid-sized manufacturers across Germany, Austria and Switzerland, on desktop and mobile. With two senior designers I designed four of its products from zero: Elly, the platform's first AI assistant; the Best Practice Hub; Documents; and Analytics. APplus won Gold in the User Experience category of ERP-System des Jahres in 2025 and again in 2026.

What I own:

The product model. The navigation, content and interaction model every APplus module follows, and the information architecture of each new application.

The design system, as code. FOX: tokens and component APIs authored in Figma, implemented in Angular, React and web components, documented in Storybook, tested in Chromatic, published to Nexus for six product teams. I author and merge component pull requests myself.

The team. Two senior product designers. Work is split by application and reviewed in a weekly critique.

Sign-off, and the work after handoff. Design acceptance is in the definition of done. I review pull requests against the frames, run design QA on the build, and reconcile the file to the code. Where a flow is easier to show than to describe, I hand over a working prototype with the Figma file, and I design components in code.

The research rhythm. Quarterly interviews, journey mapping, usability testing every release.

AI, used every day. Claude and Claude Code, Codex and Cursor for component work, refactoring and documentation. I publish agent skills that encode the design system's rules, so generated UI comes out on-system.

Before Asseco: ten years in data-dense, regulated software. The redesign of Volvo's enterprise ERP and its design system; growth features and the core design system for wolterskluwer-online.de; a production surveillance platform for upstream oil and gas at Xecta; MojePZU for insurance and healthcare; a mobile financial-education app for Riyad Bank, shipped to the App Store and Google Play.

Off screen I paint in watercolour, mostly on location, keep a field list of birds of prey, and draw plants the way botanical plates do.

Portfolio: konstancja-tanjga.github.io/portfolio-site
Big Hat, my public design system: konstancja-tanjga.github.io/bighat-design-system
